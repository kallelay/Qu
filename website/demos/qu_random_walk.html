Statistics & estimation

Random walk

a discretized Wiener-process-style random walk with a real, nonzero drift, checking that the walk's mean per-step increment converges toward the specified drift as n_steps grows (the Law of Large Numbers applied to `random_walk`'s own definition).

catalog/qu_random_walk.qu

`random_walk(n_steps, drift=, volatility=, seed=)` builds x[0]=0, x[i] = x[i-1] + drift + volatility*randn(). Since x[n] is just the sum of n i.i.d. increments each with mean `drift` and variance `volatility^2`, the sample mean increment x[n]/n has mean `drift` and standard error `volatility/sqrt(n)` -- an exact, checkable statistical property (see book/src/stdlib/statistics-ml.md's own `random_walk_final_position_variance_grows_roughly_linearly_with_step_ count` reference and the `Var(x[n]) = n*volatility^2` note).

What it exercises

  • random_walk(n_steps, drift, volatility, [seed=]) -- drift/volatility
  • are positional (confirmed against the engine's own regression tests
  • in engine/crates/qu-interp/src/lib.rs, e.g.
  • `random_walk(30, 0, 1, seed=11)`), only `seed` is named.

How it works

A walk with a real, nonzero drift and unit volatility.

true_drift = 0.05
volatility = 1.0

Try a few step counts and show the estimated per-step drift converging,

each with its own 4-standard-error band (a generous, still-checkable bound: under the CLT, a sample mean landing outside +/-4 SE happens for well under 1% of seeds).

step_counts = [1000, 100000, 4000000]
for k = 0 to length(step_counts) - 1
    n_steps = step_counts[k]
    path = random_walk(n_steps, true_drift, volatility, seed=k + 1)
    est_drift = path[n_steps] / n_steps   # path[0] = 0, so this IS the mean increment
    se = volatility / sqrt(n_steps)
    err = abs(est_drift - true_drift)
    print("n_steps={n_steps:>8}: estimated drift={est_drift:.5f}, true drift={true_drift:.5f}, |error|={err:.5f}, 4*SE={4.0*se:.5f}")
    if err < 4.0 * se then
        print("  -> within the 4-standard-error band (converging as expected).")
    else
        print("  -> MISMATCH: estimated drift is further than 4 standard errors from the truth!")
    end if
end for

Plot the smallest walk (most visually interesting -- still noisy).

small_path = random_walk(step_counts[0], true_drift, volatility, seed=1)
steps = 0 to step_counts[0]
plot(steps, small_path, label="random walk (drift={true_drift})", color="purple")
xlabel("step")
ylabel("position")
title("Random walk with drift: n_steps={step_counts[0]}")
legend()

71 programs, whole

Not fragments. Each one is a file in catalog/ that runs as it stands — a real problem from measurement, signals, or data, solved end to end. The build runs every one of them, so the printed output and the figure on each page are what the engine produced, not what someone typed.

Read one near your own work and change it. That is the fastest way into the language, and it is why these exist.

ellipse

Builtin index · Plotting

ellipse(cx, cy, rx, ry, [rotate=])

Input: cx, cy, rx, ry, rotate · Output: no output

What it is

Draws an ellipse centered at (cx, cy) (numbers) with semi-axis radii rx and ry (numbers, data units along x and y respectively). rotate= (number, degrees, anticlockwise, default 0) tilts the whole ellipse about its center.

Overloads

One call shape — this function is not overloaded.

How it works

── shapes ────────────────────────────────────────────── circle(cx, cy, r), ellipse(cx, cy, rx, ry), arc(cx, cy, r, from_deg, to_deg), polygon(xs, ys). All are sampled in DATA space and mapped through the axis transform, so a circle stays a circle when the axes are equal and becomes the correct ellipse when they are not. Emitting an SVG <circle> at a pixel radius would be simpler and would draw the wrong shape on any panel whose scales differ -- which is most of them. segments= controls the sampling; 128 is smooth at any figure size a paper uses and cheap enough not to think about.

docx.move_paragraph

Builtin index · File I/O

docx.move_paragraph(doc, from, to)

Input: doc, from, to · Output: no output

What it is

Moves body paragraph from so that it becomes body paragraph to (as numbered after the move). Tables and other block content stay where they are. A paragraph that ends a section carries the section break and is refused. *Added in v0.4.7*

Overloads

One call shape — this function is not overloaded.

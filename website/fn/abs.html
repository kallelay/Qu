abs

Builtin index · Core maths

result = abs(x)

Input: x · Output: result

What it is

Absolute value on a real scalar/Vec/Mat; magnitude (sqrt(re^2 + im^2)) on a Complex scalar, CVec, or CMat. x is the value(s) to measure.

Overloads

x can be number, Vec, or Complex.

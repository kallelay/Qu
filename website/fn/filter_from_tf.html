How it works

filter_from_tf(b, a, [fs]) -- a filter model from transfer- function coefficients in ascending powers of z^-1 (the filter(b, a, x) convention). With a feedback part the polynomials are factored and regrouped into second-order sections; with a a single number the result is an FIR model holding the taps b / a[0] as they are.

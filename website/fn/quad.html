quad

Builtin index · Signal processing

quad(f, a, b, [tol=1e-10], [maxdepth=60])

Input: f, a, b, tol, maxdepth · Output: no output

What it is

Adaptive Gauss-Kronrod (G7K15, QUADPACK's rule and error scaling) integral of f(x) over [a, b]; f takes and returns a number. a or b may be inf / -inf (a change of variable maps the range to a finite one), and a > b gives the negated integral. Endpoint singularities such as 1/sqrt(x) on (0, 1] converge because the nodes never touch the ends and the interval nearest the singularity is bisected repeatedly.

prod

Builtin index · Core maths

result = sum(x)

Input: x · Output: result

What it is

Sum / product of every element of x — a scalar, Vec, or Mat/Signal (real; sum additionally accepts a CVec/CMat and returns a Complex in that case). With no other argument, reduces to one scalar Num (or Complex). On a Mat, the optional axis= keyword (0 or 1) reduces along just that dimension instead, returning a Vec of length cols (axis=0) or rows (axis=1).

Overloads

One call shape — this function is not overloaded.

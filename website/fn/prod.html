prod

Builtin index · Core maths

result = prod(x)

Input: x · Output: result

What it is

Product of every element of x — a scalar, Vec, or Mat/Signal, real only (unlike sum, does not accept a CVec/CMat). With no other argument, reduces to one scalar Num. On a Mat, the optional axis= keyword (0 or 1) reduces along just that dimension instead, returning a Vec of length cols/rows. prod does NOT accept on_invalid= — that keyword was added to sum and the other nine reductions listed in this section's intro, not to prod.

Overloads

One call shape — this function is not overloaded.

From the documentation, and run by the build -- the output and the figure below are what the engine actually produced. Simplest first.

print("prod={prod(v)}")

print("var={var(v)}  std^2={std(v)^2}")

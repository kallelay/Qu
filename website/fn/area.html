area

Builtin index · Plotting

area(x, y)

Input: x, y · Output: no output

What it is

Filled area under the curve (x, y) (equal-length numeric Vecs) down to y=0; the zero-baseline special case of fill_between.

Overloads

One call shape — this function is not overloaded.

How it works

fill_between(x, y_lo, y_hi) — a filled band, e.g. a confidence interval; area(x, y) is the y_lo = 0 special case.

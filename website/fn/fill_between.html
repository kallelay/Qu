fill_between

Builtin index · Plotting

fill_between(x, y_lo, y_hi)

Input: x, y_lo, y_hi · Output: no output

What it is

A filled band between two curves: x, y_lo, y_hi are equal-length numeric Vecs giving the lower and upper boundary at each x, e.g. a confidence interval. It's the general form area calls into.

Overloads

One call shape — this function is not overloaded.

How it works

fill_between(x, y_lo, y_hi) — a filled band, e.g. a confidence interval; area(x, y) is the y_lo = 0 special case.

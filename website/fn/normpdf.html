normpdf

Builtin index · Statistics & ML

result = normpdf(x, [mu=0], [sigma=1])

Input: x, mu, sigma · Output: result

What it is

Gaussian probability density at x (a number or vector, elementwise) with mean mu (default 0) and standard deviation sigma (default 1).

Overloads

One call shape — this function is not overloaded.

How it works

normpdf(x, mu, sigma) — the univariate Gaussian density, elementwise over x. sigma is the standard deviation, not the variance (matches normal/randn's own parameterization).

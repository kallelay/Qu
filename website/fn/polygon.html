polygon

Builtin index · Plotting

polygon(xs, ys)

Input: xs, ys · Output: no output

What it is

Draws a closed shape connecting the points (xs[i], ys[i]) in order and back to the first. xs and ys are equal-length Vecs of numbers (data units).

Overloads

One call shape — this function is not overloaded.

How it works

polygon(xs, ys) — a closed shape through the given points. Two vectors rather than a list of pairs, because that is how every other drawing call here takes coordinates and how the data usually arrives.

From the note above this builtin in the interpreter, where the reasoning was written down as it was built.

How it works

sigma_delta(x) — a first-order (error-feedback) 1-bit noise-shaping quantizer: exact 1-bit binarization of x that pushes quantization error to higher frequencies instead of spreading it evenly (sign(x)'s own behavior) — the standard technique behind "DIBS-ΔΣ" excitation signals (multisine(...) |> sigma_delta) and 1-bit DACs generally. A signal's own Fs survives (same convention as diff/cumsum) since this doesn't change the sample rate, only re-quantizes the values.

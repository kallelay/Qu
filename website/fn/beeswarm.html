beeswarm

Builtin index · Plotting

beeswarm(data, ...), [spread=], [ms=], [marker=], [fill=]

Input: data, ... · Output: no output

What it is

Every sample drawn, nudged sideways only as far as it must be to clear its neighbours, so the WIDTH is the count. The middle ground between a box (hides the sample) and a raincloud (shows it, but scattered at random). Best below a few hundred points per group.

How it works

beeswarm(data) / beeswarm(data1, ...) -- every sample drawn, nudged sideways only as far as it must be to avoid its neighbours. The gap between a boxplot and a raincloud. A box hides the sample; a raincloud's rain shows it but scatters it at random, so density has to be read from how dark the smear is and two points can land on top of each other. A swarm makes the WIDTH the count, which is the thing being read. Best below a few hundred points per group: past that the rows saturate and a violin says the same thing in less ink.

coherence

Builtin index · Noise

result = coherence(A)

Input: A · Output: result

What it is

A is an M×N sensing matrix.

Overloads

One call shape — this function is not overloaded.

CodeInputOutputDescription
result = coherence(A)
A matrixnot documented yet
spectral_coherence —magnitude-squared spectral coherence of two signals
Returns a single number: the largest inner product between any two different normalised columns of A. Small is good. Unrelated to spectral_coherence (magnitude-squared spectral coherence of two signals) in the Signal Processing chapter's Spectral Analysis section — two different functions, two different names.

Examples

From the documentation, and run by the build -- the output and the figure below are what the engine actually produced. Simplest first.

A = randn(60, 200, seed = 7) / sqrt(60)
print("coherence {coherence(A):.3f}, provable sparsity {cs_guarantee(A)}")
output
coherence 0.558, provable sparsity 1

How it works

coherence(A) — the largest inner product between two different normalised columns of a compressed-sensing matrix. Pre-existing, single-argument, left untouched. The unrelated DSP two-signal magnitude-squared coherence lives under its own name, spectral_coherence, below — see that arm's comment for why it isn't an overload of this one.

docx.add_footnote

Builtin index · File I/O

docx.add_footnote(doc, i, text, [on=])

Input: doc, i, text, on · Output: no output

What it is

Creates a footnote with text (\n starts a new paragraph in it) anchored after the first occurrence of on in body paragraph i, or at the end of the paragraph. Word numbers footnotes by position. Creates the footnotes part, its separator notes and the note styles when the document has none; docx.footnotes reads them back. *Added in v0.4.7*

Overloads

One call shape — this function is not overloaded.

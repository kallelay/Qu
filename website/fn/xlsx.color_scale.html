xlsx.color_scale

Builtin index · File I/O

xlsx.color_scale(wb, sheet, range, [low="#F8696B"], [mid=], [high="#63BE7B"])

Input: wb, sheet, range, low, mid, high · Output: no output

What it is

Colours every cell of range on a scale from its lowest value (low) to its highest (high); with mid= a three-colour scale centred on the 50th percentile.

Overloads

One call shape — this function is not overloaded.

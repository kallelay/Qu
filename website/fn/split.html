split

Builtin index · Collections & strings

result = split(s, delim, [limit])

Input: s, delim, limit · Output: result

What it is

s and delim are Strs; optional limit (number of pieces, default: unlimited) stops after that many pieces and leaves the remainder undivided as the last one — split(line, ": ", 2) takes key: value with: colons apart that way.

Overloads

One call shape — this function is not overloaded.

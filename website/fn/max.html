max

Builtin index · Core maths

max(x, [on_invalid=]) or max(a, b)

Input: x, on_invalid · Output: no output

What it is

One argument reduces x (a scalar, Vec, or Mat/Signal) to its largest/smallest element, a scalar Num, and accepts on_invalid= ("propagate"/"ignore"/"error", see this section's intro). Two arguments a, b (matching or broadcastable shapes) instead compare elementwise and keep the larger/smaller of each pair — the "clip" idiom, e.g. max(x, 0); on_invalid= does not apply to this two-argument form. In the one-argument form on a Mat, the optional axis= keyword (0 or 1) reduces along just that dimension instead, returning a length-cols/rows Vec rather than a single scalar.

Overloads

x can be scalar, Vec, Mat, Signal, or Num; a can be scalar, Vec, Mat, Signal, or Num.

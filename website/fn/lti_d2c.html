Returns the continuous system that lti_c2d with the same method would turn into the discrete sys, in state-space form. method: "zoh" or "tustin". A zero-order-hold equivalent needs a real matrix logarithm, which does not exist when the system has a pole at zero or on the negative real axis; a Tustin equivalent does not exist for a pole at z = -1. Both cases are errors.

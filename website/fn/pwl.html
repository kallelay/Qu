pwl

Builtin index · Plotting

pwl(x, y, [n_segments])

Input: x, y, n_segments · Output: no output

What it is

Continuous piecewise-linear least-squares fit through equal-length numeric Vecs x, y. n_segments (integer, positional, default 4) sets how many straight segments approximate the data; plotted through its own fitted breakpoints.

Overloads

One call shape — this function is not overloaded.

How it works

pwl(x, y, [n_segments]) -- continuous piecewise-linear least-squares fit (default 4 segments), plotted directly as the polyline through its own breakpoints (see pwl_fit).

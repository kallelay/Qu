measure_snr

Builtin index · Noise

result = measure_snr(clean, noisy)

Input: clean, noisy · Output: result

What it is

Computes the achieved signal-to-noise ratio by treating noisy - clean as the noise. clean and noisy are two length-N number vectors of equal length.

Overloads

One call shape — this function is not overloaded.

How it works

measure_snr(clean, noisy) — what the SNR actually is, taking the noise as the difference. The loop-closer: it lets "add noise at 3 dB" be checked rather than trusted. Not called snr, which already means the spectral single-argument estimate.

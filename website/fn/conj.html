conj

Builtin index · Core maths

result = conj(x)

Input: x · Output: result

What it is

Complex conjugate (negates the imaginary part); passes a real x through unchanged. x is a scalar, Vec, or Mat (real or complex).

Overloads

x can be scalar, Vec, Mat, or Complex.

panel

Builtin index · Plotting

panel(rows, cols, k)

Input: rows, cols, k · Output: no output

What it is

Selects cell k (integer, 1-based, row-major) of a rows-by-cols (integers) grid as the target for further drawing. subplot is the same call under MATLAB's name.

Overloads

One call shape — this function is not overloaded.

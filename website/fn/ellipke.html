ellipke

Builtin index · Core maths

ellipke(m)

Input: m · Output: no output

What it is

Complete elliptic integrals of the first and second kind with parameter m = k², returned as a record {K, E} shaped like m (number, Vec or Mat): ellipke(0) has K = E = pi/2, ellipke(0.5) has K = 1.8540746773013719 and E = 1.3506438810476755. Computed with the arithmetic-geometric mean; against mpmath the worst relative error is 5e-15 over m from -100 to 0.999999, so negative m and m close to 1 are covered. m = 1 gives K = inf, E = 1; m > 1 or NaN give NaN. Complex input is an error.

Overloads

One call shape — this function is not overloaded.

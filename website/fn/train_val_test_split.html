train_val_test_split

Builtin index · Statistics & ML

result = train_val_test_split(X, y, [val_size=0.2], [test_size=0.2], [seed=], [shuffle=true])

Input: X, y, val_size, test_size, seed, shuffle · Output: result

What it is

Three-way split of X/y into training, validation, and test sets: val_size/test_size are numbers in (0, 1) (both default 0.2); val_size is rescaled against the remainder left after removing the test set, so the final validation share really is what you asked for rather than a share of a share.

Overloads

One call shape — this function is not overloaded.

How it works

train_val_test_split(X, y, [val_size=0.2], [test_size=0.2], [seed=], [shuffle=true]) — the three-way split hyperparameter tuning needs: a validation set to select/tune on plus a final held-out test set that's never touched until the very end. Implemented as two sequential splits done carefully: X/y are permuted once (same seed=/shuffle= contract as train_test_split), test_size peels the trailing test fraction off the whole dataset first, and val_size then peels its share off the REMAINING (1-test_size) pool — rescaled to val_size / (1-test_size) of that remainder, not val_size of it directly, so the *final* validation fraction of the whole dataset actually equals val_size as requested. (The naive version — val_size of what's left after removing the test set, unrescaled — silently under-delivers: e.g. val_size=0.2 after a test_size=0.2 cut leaves a pool of 80% of the data, and taking a naive 20% of *that* pool is only 16% of the original dataset, not the requested 20%.) Returns a "split3" model handle with .X_train/.X_val/.X_test/ .y_train/.y_val/.y_test fields.

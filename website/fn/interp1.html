interp1

Builtin index · Signal processing

result = interp1(x, y, xq, [method="linear"|"spline"|"nearest"])

Input: x, y, xq, method · Output: result

What it is

x, y: matching length-N vectors (the known points, x strictly increasing); xq: a number or length-M vector, the query point(s); method (optional string). 1-D interpolation through (x, y) (MATLAB's interp1). Extrapolation beyond x's range is always on — "linear" continues the boundary segment's own slope, "spline" extends the natural cubic spline's boundary segment, "nearest" just keeps returning the nearest endpoint's y — never a clamp or an error, matching what "interpolation *and* extrapolation" implies.

Overloads

The method string picks between three genuinely different curve shapes through the same six points — worth plotting side by side rather than trusting the names alone.

xq can be number or vector.

How it works

interp1(x, y, xq, [method]) — method is "linear" (default, extrapolates via the boundary segment's slope), "spline" (natural cubic, shares splineplot's solver), or "nearest". xq may be a scalar or a vector; extrapolation beyond x's range is always on (there's no separate flag to opt in — a query point outside the data is still answered, never an error), matching what "interpolation *and* extrapolation" implies.

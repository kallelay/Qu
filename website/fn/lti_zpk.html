lti_zpk

dt optionaldt as in lti_ss.
result vector, number, or Complex
Returns the single-input single-output system k * prod(s - z_i) / prod(s - p_i) as an "lti" model. z, p: vectors of zeros and poles, real or complex ([] for none); k: number; dt as in lti_ss. Complex zeros and poles must come in conjugate pairs, and there may not be more zeros than poles.

How it works

db_power(x) — the POWER-domain counterpart to db above, 10*log10(power_ratio): an alias for pow2db(x), for the same "SciPy/general habit reaches for this name" reason. Do NOT confuse with db/mag2db: swapping the factor of 10 vs. 20 here is the classic amplitude-vs-power dB mistake — db is for field quantities (V, A, Pa, ...), db_power is for quantities that are already a power/energy ratio (W, W/Hz, |X|^2, ...).

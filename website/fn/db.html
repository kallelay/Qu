How it works

db(x) — a bare, SciPy/general-DSP-flavored alias for mag2db(x) above: 20*log10(x), the AMPLITUDE/field-quantity convention (voltage, current, pressure, ...). mag2db/ db2mag stay the canonical names (see their own doc comment for why a single unqualified db() was deliberately left out originally) — this is only here because callers coming from SciPy/plain-DSP habit reach for a bare db() first, and it is clearer to answer that expectation explicitly, spelling out which convention it picked, than to leave it erroring.

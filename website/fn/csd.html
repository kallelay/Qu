How it works

csd(x, y, fs, [nperseg=], [noverlap=], [window="hann"]) — cross-spectral density Pxy of x and y, Welch's method, sharing welch's own framing/windowing/averaging exactly (numeric::transforms::csd) so it lines up bin-for-bin with welch(x, ...)/welch(y, ...). Returns the one-sided complex CSD as a CVec (nperseg/2 + 1 bins, DC to Nyquist) — abs(csd(...)) is the cross-power magnitude, angle(csd(...)) the phase; spectral_coherence below is the normalized |Pxy|^2/(Pxx*Pyy) derived from this plus two welch calls.

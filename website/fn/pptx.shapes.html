pptx.shapes

Builtin index · File I/O

pptx.shapes(p, i)

Input: p, i · Output: no output

What it is

The slide's top-level shapes, back to front, as records with id, name, kind (text, placeholder, shape, picture, table, chart, graphic, group, connector), placeholder (its type, or none), x, y, w, h in mm (a placeholder that sets no position reports the one it inherits from its layout or master) and text. The other shape functions take a shape as this id (stable across saves) or its name; a group is one shape. *Added in v0.4.5*

Overloads

One call shape — this function is not overloaded.

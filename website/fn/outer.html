outer

Builtin index · Core maths

outer(u, v)

Input: u, v · Output: no output

What it is

Outer product u·vᵀ: a len(u)×len(v) Mat of u[i]·v[j], whatever the orientation of the two vectors. Empty or complex input is an error.

Overloads

One call shape — this function is not overloaded.

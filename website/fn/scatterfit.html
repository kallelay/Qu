scatterfit

Builtin index · Plotting

scatterfit(x, y, [degree])

Input: x, y, degree · Output: no output

What it is

Draws a scatter of equal-length numeric Vecs x, y plus a polyfit trend line. degree (integer, positional, default 1) is the polynomial degree, sampled densely over x's range.

Overloads

One call shape — this function is not overloaded.

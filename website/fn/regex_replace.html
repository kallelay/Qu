regex_replace

Builtin index · Collections & strings

result = regex_replace(s, pattern, repl, [count])

Input: s, pattern, repl, count · Output: result

What it is

s, pattern and repl are Strs; optional count (number, default: all occurrences) caps how many replacements are made. $1 and ${name} in repl refer to capture groups.

Overloads

One call shape — this function is not overloaded.

How it works

regex_replace(s, pattern, replacement, [count]) — $1 and ${name} in the replacement refer to capture groups. Every occurrence unless count says otherwise.

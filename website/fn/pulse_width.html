Builtin index · Signal processing

Returns a single number: the mean width of the complete pulses, in seconds for a Signal and samples otherwise. Equal by construction to mean(find_pulses(x, ...).widths).

How it works

pulse_width(x, [level=], [hysteresis=], [polarity="positive"]) -- the MEAN width of the complete pulses in x, in seconds for a Signal and samples otherwise. Equal by construction to mean(find_pulses(x, ...).widths), because it is the same computation.

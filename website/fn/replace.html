replace

Builtin index · Collections & strings

result = replace(s, old, new)

Input: s, old, new · Output: result

What it is

s is the Str to search; old and new are Strs. Every literal (non-regex) occurrence of old in s is replaced by new.

Overloads

One call shape — this function is not overloaded.

Examples

From the documentation, and run by the build -- the output and the figure below are what the engine actually produced. Simplest first.

replace("axb a.b", "a.b", "Z")                     # "axb Z"   the dot is a dot

replace("axb a.b", "a.b", "Z", pattern="regex")    # "Z Z"

replace("a1 b2 c3", "?#", "X", pattern="wildcard") # "X X X"
